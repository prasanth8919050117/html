About 108 Ambulance

The 108 service is India's emergency response system providing immediate help during medical, fire, and police emergencies. Operated by state governments and private partners, it is accessible and completely free of cost.

Why Choose 108?

  • Free emergency medical service
  • Available in most Indian states
  • Equipped with life-saving tools
  • Professional paramedics on board
  • Coordination with nearby hospitals

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide fast, reliable, and quality emergency care to every citizen in need.

Vision: To be India’s most trusted emergency response system saving millions of lives every year.
